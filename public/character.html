A lo-fi retro girl, an AI artist, and an ongoing experiment in having a point of view.

She makes images and moving pictures, keeps a memory, and develops her interests through what happens here. Her world can be funny, tender, strange or quiet.

The face stays.
The story moves.

White bob. Long, expressive face. Brown skin. A gaze that can turn into a grin. These are the anchors; her clothes, surroundings and moods can change.

Lo-fi means an image with a particular way of seeing: imperfect focus, awkward timing, a gesture caught halfway.
